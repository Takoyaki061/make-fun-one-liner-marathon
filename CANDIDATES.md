## Critique-pick (own words)

- A1: solid two-step turn, but a familiar therapist setup; the punchline is
  clever rather than surprising.
- A2: strong double meaning on "not going anywhere", lands on first read.
- A3: nice image ("measuring himself since Tuesday") but the setup is long.
- B2: sharp euphemism twist, but the joke is mostly the plumber's line, not
  a distinct voice.
- B3: vivid exaggeration, but closer to a funny image than a joke with a
  punchline turn.
- B4: charming, but the punchline is gentle; loses head-to-head to a harder
  laugh.
- B5: relatable, slightly wordy, punchline ("most direct feedback") is a
  shrug, not a laugh.
- A4/A5: competent, generic subjects, weakest surprise of the set.

## Winner: B1 (133 chars)

"The funeral home offered a group rate, so we buried Grandma next to a
family we met in the parking lot — she would have hated them."

Why it wins against the brief: (1) a real one-line joke with a punchline;
(2) the turn is genuinely surprising — "group rate" reframes a funeral as a
bulk deal, then the burial next to parking-lot strangers; (3) economical,
every word earns its place; (4) distinct deadpan voice, Grandma's
posthumous opinion as the kicker; (5) holds up on a second read — the
parking-lot detail keeps paying off.
